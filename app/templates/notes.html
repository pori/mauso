Notes

Saved notes the AI can look up with the search_notes skill when you ask it to reference something you've written down here. Unlike conversation history, these are not end-to-end encrypted -- they're stored in plaintext on the server so the backend can search them, the same tradeoff as uploaded Files. Don't put anything here you wouldn't put in any other self-hosted app on this box. (This is separate from the AI's own in-chat "note" replies, which stay in the encrypted conversation and are never saved here.)
